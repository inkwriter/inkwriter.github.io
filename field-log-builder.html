Panels — reading order

Fold
  1. Print landscape at actual size — 100% scale, not “fit to page.”
  2. Fold in half the long way (hot dog), crease, open flat.
  3. Fold in half short way, then again — eight rectangles.
  4. Cut the dashed center line, middle two panels only.
  5. Fold long way again, push the ends together, wrap into a booklet.
